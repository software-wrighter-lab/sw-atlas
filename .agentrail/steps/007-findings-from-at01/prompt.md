demo-decision-model ran sw-atlas's own problem on sw-atlas's own data (their step 024, lesson AT01, commit b78a2e1) and reported four findings that change this project's plan. Read docs/experiments/AT01-atlas-rerank.md and docs/reference/downstream-requests.md in ../../sw-ml-study/demo-decision-model first. (1) A defect in lib/scorer.mlpl this project would have vendored: unit normalization guarded the zero case after sqrt, which is correct forward and NaN backward, so one card whose every word is unknown turns every parameter into NaN on the first Adam step -- silently, printing accuracy 0.000 with MRR exactly 1.000. Their catalog of twelve hand-written cards never had such a card; ours has 642. Fixed upstream with an epsilon inside the root, recorded as their finding Q6 with a probe. Record that the vendor must be taken at or after that commit. (2) The rerank head does not work at this data scale: reranking the top 20 it scored 0.023 warm and 0.032 cold against 0.050 for random, with training loss 0.011 -- 174 training questions memorized, nothing transferred, and warm equals cold so this is not the held-out cards failing. The design is unfunded rather than refuted: what decides it is how many labelled questions per resource are needed, and their proposed next experiment is synthetic positives from each card's own title and summary to teach the shared space before fine-tuning on real questions. (3) The hybrid's ceiling is the matcher's recall at k: 0.63 at k=20 on our sets with their stand-in matcher, so no reranker can exceed that there, and a larger k is a harder reranking problem rather than an easier one. Our MB02 is not their stand-in and this project owes its own recall-at-k number. (4) Intent and the Nouls beat nothing: five-class intent 0.697 against a 0.737 always-FindResource baseline, the is-off-topic Noul exactly at its always-false baseline, because 72% of our questions are FindResource -- a set drafted to test destinations does not teach intent. Consequences to write down: the eval harness must report the majority-class baseline beside every accuracy and never a bare accuracy; Saga 3 needs a synthetic-positives step before the rerank head; the question sets need an intent-balanced supplement, which is owner work since he confirmed the existing ones; and MB02's own recall at k is now the most important number Saga 2 produces.
