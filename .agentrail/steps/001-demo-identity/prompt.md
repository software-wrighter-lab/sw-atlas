Two faults found while sampling the corpus for question drafting, both in how a declared link becomes a resource, and both invisible to the coverage gates because the resources involved are related and therefore not orphans. (1) atlas_core::link_id trims a trailing slash but not a trailing fragment, so the campus is declared twice -- demo:software-wrighter-lab.github.io/sw-campus and the same id with '/#' -- and a matcher would score against a duplicate. Normalize the tail properly: strip a trailing fragment and any trailing slashes, repeatedly, and pin it with tests including the real pair. (2) Demo and video resources take their titles from the markdown link text a declarer wrote, so the corpus holds demos titled 'run' and 'io demo', which are labels on a button rather than names of a thing. Treat a generic label as no title -- a small committed list, documented, not a guess -- and leave the title empty rather than inventing one, since an empty title is honest and the resolver can show the declaring resource's name instead. File the underlying ask where a reader of the affected repository would look: the campus and the blog are the only places that know what these demos are called. Exit: no duplicate identifiers in the corpus, no resource whose title is a button label, counts updated wherever they are quoted, just check green.
